3. Open your Downloads folder once the file finishes saving.

5. Follow the prompts on your screen to finish the installation.

## 💡 Key Features

KubaOS acts as a central hub for your professional life. We designed the tools to save you time and decrease mental clutter.

### AI Automation
The app integrates with AI models like Claude to help you write emails, draft business plans, and organize project data. You trigger these tools directly from the main workspace by typing simple commands.

### Local-First Data Storage
Your data lives on your hard drive. This ensures that you have access to your documents even when you lack an internet connection. The application syncs your information to the cloud only when you request it.

### Focus Management
The interface removes distractions. You can hide notifications, block specific sites while working, and track your time against different projects. This creates a dedicated environment for deep work.

### Messaging and Communication
You link your common communication channels to your workspace. KubaOS pulls messages into a unified feed so you avoid switching between tabs. Use this to sort urgent customer requests from internal team updates.

Because KubaOS runs on your machine, you maintain control over your files. The app sends no user data to external servers without your explicit permission. We designed the storage system to prevent unauthorized access by encrypting your local database. Always keep your Windows installation updated to ensure the best security for your hardware.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Does KubaOS work on Mac or Linux?**
Currently, we only support Windows desktop environments.

**How do I update the application?**
The app checks for updates every time you launch it. If a new version exists, the software prompts you to download and install the update automatically.

**Can I use this without an internet connection?**
Yes. You can create, edit, and organize files while offline. The app saves all changes locally and updates your cloud accounts once you reconnect.

**Where does the software store my data?**
By default, the application creates a folder in your Documents library. You can change this location during the initial setup or via the Settings menu at any time.

**What happens if I delete a project?**
Deleted projects move to a local trash folder. You can recover these items for up to 30 days before the software permanently removes them from your drive.

**Does this software cost money?**
The core functionality is free for individual founders. We plan to release enterprise features later for larger teams that require multi-user management.
